About

Research on sensor fusion and localization.

I’m Henry, a research assistant working on sensor fusion and localization in challenging, GNSS-denied environments.

My work sits at the intersection of visual-inertial navigation, geospatial perception, and aerial robotics. I develop practical methods and datasets that help autonomous systems understand where they are — even when conventional positioning is unreliable.
